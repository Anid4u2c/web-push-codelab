Welcome to the push messaging "enhanced" codelab. Push the button below to enable/disable push messages.

Enter your push server's VAPID public key below to subscribe. The original Glitch companion site is no longer hosted. A replacement message sender is not yet available.

Your browser is subscribed. To send a message, your application server needs this subscription and the private VAPID key matching the public key used above.

You can copy the subscription for your sender. Treat subscription details as private and do not publish them.

Notification icons and actions depend on browser and operating-system support.
